The Dual-Track Strategy

This section illustrates the core architectural approach of Bharat Bodh. To effectively mainstream Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS), the project abandons a monolithic approach in favor of a dual-track delivery system. It simultaneously targets elite academic rigor and broad public awareness, ensuring both deep scholarship and wide democratization of knowledge.

📚

⚔ Track 1: Academic Rigor

Core Focus: University Ecosystems

  • ▶ High-level seminars & conferences challenging Eurocentric frameworks.
  • ▶ Republication of the 14-volume 'History of Indian Science and Technology' (HIST).
  • ▶ Scholar Mentorship program cultivating 200+ PhDs and young researchers.
🚐

📡 Track 2: Mobile Awareness

Auxiliary Focus: Campus Democratization

  • ▶ 14-seater solar-hybrid Mobile Heritage Library Van deploying directly to colleges.
  • ▶ Distribution of bilingual, accessible, jargon-free booklets and materials.
  • ▶ Digital kiosks and outdoor AV canopy for massive student engagement.
